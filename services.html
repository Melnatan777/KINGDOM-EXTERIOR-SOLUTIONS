Service 01

Pressure Washing

Over time, driveways, sidewalks and concrete surfaces collect dirt, oil stains, mildew and algae that regular cleaning can't touch. Our high-powered pressure washing equipment blasts away years of buildup and restores surfaces to like-new condition.

We adjust water pressure based on the surface type to ensure thorough cleaning without causing damage. The results are immediate and dramatic — your driveway will look like the day it was poured.

Service 02

House Washing

The exterior of your home is constantly exposed to the Houston elements — humidity, algae, mildew, pollen and pollution. Over time this buildup dulls your home's appearance and can damage siding and paint if left untreated.

We use a low-pressure soft wash method that safely removes contaminants from any siding type — vinyl, brick, stucco, wood or fiber cement — without the risk of damage that high-pressure washing can cause on exterior walls.

Service 03

Roof Cleaning

Those dark black streaks on your roof aren't just unsightly — they're a type of algae called Gloeocapsa Magma that feeds on your shingles and shortens the life of your roof. Left untreated, it spreads and causes real structural damage over time.

We use a no-pressure soft wash treatment — the method recommended by shingle manufacturers — that kills the algae at the root and restores your roof's appearance without voiding your warranty or damaging shingles.

What's Included

  • Asphalt shingle roofs
  • Tile roofs
  • Metal roofs
  • Moss & lichen removal
  • Algae & black streak removal
  • Manufacturer-safe process

Service 04

Surface Cleaning

Standard pressure washing can leave streaks and uneven results on large flat surfaces. Our surface cleaner attachment spins water at high speed across the entire surface evenly — delivering a cleaner, streak-free result in a fraction of the time.

Ideal for large concrete or paver areas. Great for HOA compliance, pre-listing preparation or just keeping your property looking sharp year-round.

Common Questions

What Homeowners Ask Us

How long does a typical job take?

Most residential jobs are completed in 1–3 hours depending on the size of the property. We'll give you a time estimate upfront.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to the areas and a water source, many homeowners aren't home. We communicate before and after.

Is pressure washing safe for my plants?

Yes — we protect your landscaping, rinse surrounding areas and use biodegradable cleaning agents where needed.

How often should I have my home washed?

In the Houston climate, most homeowners benefit from exterior cleaning once a year, sometimes twice for roofs and driveways.

Will roof cleaning damage my shingles?

No. We use a low-pressure soft wash that is manufacturer-approved and won't void your roof warranty.
