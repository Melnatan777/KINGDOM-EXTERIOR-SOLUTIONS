Who We Are

A Company Built on Something Greater

Kingdom Exterior Solutions was founded by Tanner with a simple but powerful idea: that a service company could operate with genuine Christian values — honesty, humility and a relentless commitment to doing things right.

The name "Kingdom" isn't just a brand. It's a daily reminder of the standard we hold ourselves to. We believe that the work we do on your home is a reflection of the character we carry, and we take that seriously on every single job.

We're locally owned, Houston-based and proud to serve the communities we live in. When you hire Kingdom Exterior Solutions, you're not calling a franchise or a faceless corporation — you're calling a neighbor who genuinely cares about your property and your experience.
